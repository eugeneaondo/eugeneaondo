### About

I work on the parts of software people rarely see but always depend on: the APIs, the data stores, and the pipelines that move information between them. I care about systems that are quiet in production, well documented, and easy for the next engineer to pick up.

**What keeps me curious**

- Designing backends and data models that scale without drama
- Time-series and IoT data, from the sensor all the way to the dashboard
- Payments and integrations, where correctness matters more than cleverness
- Making slow queries fast, and fast systems observable
- Turning rough ideas into working products

**Currently exploring:** retrieval over structured data, vector search inside Postgres, and event-driven pipelines.
